Collective Care

Professional Cleaning & Groundskeeping Solutions for Student Accommodations, Commercial Properties, and Residential Homes. Tailored packages and flexible custom quotes designed around your exact needs.

Student Accommodation & Property Tiers

Structured maintenance plans designed to keep student residences spotless, hygienic, and fully compliant year-round.

Tier 1

Essential Care

Best for small student residences (10–15 beds), small office suites, or boutique properties requiring basic routine upkeep.

  • Schedule: 3 Days per week (Flex visits).
  • Staffing: 1 Cleaner / Groundsman (shared flexible shifts).
  • Interior: Sweeping, mopping, bathroom sanitization, shared kitchen wiping, trash removal.
  • Exterior/Gardening: Grass cutting, edge trimming, courtyard sweeping, and boundary litter collection.
  • Equipment & Supplies: 100% provided by Collective Care (mower, tools, chemicals).
Retainer: R4,800 – R5,200 / mo
Tier 2

Enhanced Care

Best for medium student houses (20–35 beds), multi-unit student communes, or busy commercial setups.

  • Schedule: 3 to 4 Days per week.
  • Staffing: 2–3 Staff (Dedicated cleaning team + gardener).
  • Interior: Deep clean of high-traffic communal kitchens, shared ablutions, corridors, & windows.
  • Exterior/Gardening: Mowing, weeding, pruning, gutter debris clearing, & paving pressure washing.
  • Event Support: Fast-response cleanup after student gatherings.
Retainer: R6,500 – R7,500 / mo
Tier 3

Full-Scale Care

Best for large student accommodation complexes (40–60+ beds) needing continuous on-site presence.

  • Schedule: 5 Days a week (Monday to Friday, full-time).
  • Staffing: 1 or more Full-Time Cleaners + 1 Full-Time Groundsman (+ float cover).
  • Interior: Daily continuous sanitization, spill response, & monthly appliance deep cleans.
  • Turnover Care: On-demand event cleanup + end-of-semester room turnaround assistance.
  • Equipment: All commercial tools, industrial chemicals, & waste disposal gear included.
Retainer: R9,800 – R11,500 / mo

Household Cleaning Tiers

Reliable residential housekeeping services designed for working professionals, apartments, and family homes.

Standard

Standard Refresh

Best for busy working professionals, apartments, or small families needing consistent neatness.

  • Schedule: Bi-weekly (Twice a month).
  • Staffing: 1 Professional Residential Cleaner.
  • Living/Bedrooms: Dusting, sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping.
  • Kitchen & Bathrooms: Countertops, sink sanitization, toilet, shower, and mirror scrubbing.
  • Supplies: 100% provided by Collective Care.
R450 – R650 / visit
Popular

Deep Care

Best for standard family homes or medium-sized houses requiring thorough weekly maintenance.

  • Schedule: Once a week (4 times a month).
  • Staffing: 1 Dedicated Cleaner (or team of 2).
  • Includes: Everything in Standard Refresh plus window sills, skirting boards, door handles, & bed-making.
  • Deep Scrub: Thorough kitchen and bathroom deep-scrubbing.
  • Outdoor: Courtyard or patio entrance sweeping.
R550 – R750 / visit
Premium

Premium Total Care

Best for large family homes or homeowners wanting a completely hands-off housekeeping experience.

  • Schedule: Weekly visits + rotational specialized deep cleans.
  • Staffing: 2 Professional Cleaners.
  • Includes: Everything in Tier 1 & 2 plus rotational oven/fridge deep cleans.
  • Add-ons: Weekly ironing and laundry washing/hanging support.
  • Priority: Flexible rescheduling & priority seasonal booking slots.
R950 – R1,300 / visit
